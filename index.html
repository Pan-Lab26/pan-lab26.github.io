Research

Functional Materials

We design selective adsorbents and study how their structure and surface chemistry govern ion uptake.

Hydrometallurgy

We develop aqueous separation processes and study how they perform with complex feedstocks and under continuous operation.

Critical Mineral Recovery

We recover lithium, rare earth elements, and other valuable metals from unconventional resources and waste streams.
